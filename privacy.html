11. SMS Communications and Mobile Information

When you provide your mobile phone number and consent to SMS communications, Redline Data Lab may send you informational text messages about appointment reminders, onboarding, account or service updates, and customer support.

Message Frequency: Message frequency varies based on your service requests and account activity.

Message and Data Rates: Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details.

Opting Out: You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message from us. After opting out, you will no longer receive SMS messages from Redline Data Lab.

Help: For assistance, reply HELP to any message from us or contact us at hello@redline-data.xyz.

Consent Not Required: SMS consent is not a condition of purchase. You may use our services without consenting to receive SMS messages.

Mobile Information Collection: We collect mobile phone numbers only when voluntarily provided by you. We do not collect mobile numbers without your explicit consent.

Consent Recording: When you consent to SMS messages, we record your consent timestamp, source URL, disclosure version, and exact disclosure text as evidence of your consent.

Third-Party Sharing: Redline Data Lab does not sell or share mobile numbers or SMS consent records with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
